Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Word Search Scenic Journey (the "Application"), developed and published by Volis Games ("we", "us", or "our").

1. Information Collection and Use

The Application is designed as an offline word puzzle game. We do not collect, store, or transmit any sensitive personal identification information, such as your real name, phone number, physical address, contact list, camera feeds, or microphone recordings.

Game data such as solved levels, best completion times, unlocked themes, postcards, and hint counts are stored locally on your device's internal storage and are never uploaded to our servers.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Application displays advertisements to support ongoing development. We integrate the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), which may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers and device data in accordance with Google's policies.

Information processed by Google AdMob may include:

  • IP address and approximate coarse location.
  • Advertising ID (Android AAID) for ad attribution and frequency capping.
  • Device performance metrics and crash diagnostic reports.

For more details on how Google uses this data, review the Google Ad & Content Network Privacy Policy.

3. Children's Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. All advertising requests comply with Google Play Families Policies and applicable child privacy regulations.

4. Security

We prioritize the protection of local game data and do not maintain external databases containing personal player profiles.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect game updates or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated effective date.
