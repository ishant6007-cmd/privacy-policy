Last updated: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications, games, and related services ("Games") developed and published by Volis Games ("we", "us", or "our").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information collected through our Games. This policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and how it is protected.

Our Games do not collect personally identifiable information such as your real name, home address, physical location, or contact list.

Our Games may display advertisements served by third-party ad networks to keep our content free to play. These partners may use device identifiers and cookies to serve personalized or contextual advertisements in accordance with their privacy policies.

3. Children's Privacy

Our Games are designed for a general audience and are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We do not store your personal data on external private servers. Any game progress or settings are stored locally on your device or via standard cloud save integrations. If you wish to reset or delete local game data, you can do so at any time by clearing the app's cache/storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the Game.

5. Security

All data transmissions conducted by integrated third-party SDKs (such as AdMob) are encrypted in transit using standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.
